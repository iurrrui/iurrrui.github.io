A prototype text editor that generates a node-link diagram and a timeline according to the text input. Architext allows people to construct non-sequential text structures by applying a syntax based on Markdown. It was created for an experiment that involved testing to what extent constructing different visual renditions of content can facilitate text comprehension.

Using social contract theory as sample content, the video below demonstrates one way information can be organized in Architext.
